1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit websites. They help sites remember information about your visit.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for:

  • Essential site functionality
  • Preferences (like dark mode)
  • Anonymous analytics
  • Security features

3. Types of Cookies

Necessary Cookies

Required for basic functions. Cannot be switched off.

Preference Cookies

Remember your display settings and preferences.

Analytics Cookies

Help us understand how visitors use our site.

4. Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling cookies may affect functionality.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookies Policy. The "last updated" date will reflect changes.

We use cookies to enhance your experience. By continuing, you agree to our Cookies Policy.
