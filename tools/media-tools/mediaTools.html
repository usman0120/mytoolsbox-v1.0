Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our tools

Are my files uploaded to your servers?

No, all processing happens directly in your browser. Your files never leave your device, ensuring complete privacy and security.

What file formats do you support?

We support all common media formats including MP4, MP3, WAV, FLAC, OGG, AVI, MOV, and more. Each tool specifies the supported formats.

Is there a file size limit?

For best performance, we recommend files under 500MB. Larger files may work but could take longer to process depending on your device.

Do I need to install anything?

No installation required! All our tools work directly in your web browser on any device.

Are these tools really free?

Yes, all our tools are completely free to use with no hidden charges or watermarks on your files.
